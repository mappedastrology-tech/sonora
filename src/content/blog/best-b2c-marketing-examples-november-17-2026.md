These are the ten consumer marketing ideas I'd steal this week. Each one ends with the move you can take into your own next campaign.

## 1. He told shoppers his scarves looked bad

![The Namdaemun Market scarf seller at his stall in Seoul](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/scarf-seller.png)

![Crowds filming outside the scarf shop at Namdaemun Market](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/scarf-crowd.png)

A scarf seller at Seoul's Namdaemun Market went viral for telling customers which scarves didn't suit them. Clips of him [spread across Korean social media](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10924116), crowds packed the alley outside his shop, and his online store sold out. [Herald Business reported](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10925871) that the crowds got big enough that he paused business for safety.

**Steal this:** say who your product isn't for. Honesty about fit makes the yes more believable.

## 2. Oreo made Santa petty

![Santa soaks his feet while eating Oreos in Oreo's holiday ad](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/oreo.webp)

In [Oreo's first new holiday ad in five years](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2026/11/13/oreo-christmas-ad-five-years/), a dad eats the cookies left out for Santa, and Santa gets revenge. [Marketing Dive has the details](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/oreo-refreshes-holiday-creative-with-spin-on-santas-milk-and-cookies/874493/) on how the brand reworked the milk-and-cookies tradition.

**Steal this:** make your product the punchline of the joke, so nobody can retell it without naming you.

## 3. Meow Mix wrote itself into three famous cat memes

![A still from Meow Mix's Behind the Memeow campaign](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/meow-mix.webp)

Meow Mix's ["Behind the Memeow" campaign](https://adage.com/creativity/work/aa-meow-mix-behind-the-memeow/) invents origin stories for Keyboard Cat, Table Cat and Huh Cat, each with new words set to the Meow Mix jingle. [Marketing Dive covers the rollout](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/meow-mix-reimagines-iconic-cat-memes-for-multichannel-campaign/874724/) across channels.

**Steal this:** borrow the memes your buyers already share. You skip the work of getting people to care.

## 4. Your internet company wants you offline

![John C. Reilly in Xfinity's holiday ad](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/xfinity.jpg)

In [Xfinity's holiday spot](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/xfinity-conjures-john-c-reilly-to-get-you-off-wi-fi-this-holiday-season/), John C. Reilly plays the Ghost of Christmas Very-Recent-Past. The ad tells families to put their phones down, then [shows the feature that pauses the house WiFi](https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20261108846863/xfinity-wants-you-to-pause-your-wifi-this-holiday-season) with one tap.

**Steal this:** sell the feature that fixes a problem your category causes. Admitting the problem earns you the right to sell the fix.

## 5. A jar that counts your family dinners

![A woman holds a Rao's jar labeled with the meals she has left with family](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/raos.webp)

Rao's handed people jars labeled with how many meals they have left with their family members, part of a [four-minute film from Goodby Silverstein & Partners](https://news.designrush.com/raos-homemade-goodby-silverstein-partners-limited-time-only). [Marketing Dive explains](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/raos-heats-up-brand-building-with-a-request-to-slow-down-meals/874122/) why a premium sauce is asking people to slow down.

**Steal this:** sell the moment, not the jar. A premium price is easier to accept when you know what it's for.

## 6. Rick Astley sang a duet with Dexi the cow

![Rick Astley sings to Dexi the cow in Black Cow's ad](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/black-cow.jpg)

Black Cow vodka is made from milk, so its ad has [Rick Astley sing with Dexi the cow](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/rick-astley-s-unexpected-duet-with-dexi) in a parody of a BBC radio weather report. [LBB has the background](https://lbbonline.com/news/rick-astley-black-cow-rsa) on Astley's role with the brand.

**Steal this:** give a celebrity a joke only your brand can tell. Any vodka can hire a star, but only a milk vodka gets the cow.

## 7. The burger comes with a deck box

![Burger King Italy's Magic: The Gathering meal with deck box and card sleeves](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/burger-king.jpg)

Burger King Italy's [Magic: The Gathering meal](https://www.dexerto.com/food/burger-king-reveals-22-magic-the-gathering-meal-with-deck-boxes-and-mtg-card-sleeves-3426959/) comes with a deck box and 100 card sleeves, and nine restaurants host tournament qualifiers. [Players lined up and resold the bundles](https://www.smartworld.it/news/burger-king-x-magic-scatena-il-caos.html) within hours.

**Steal this:** give fans gear they would buy anyway, and the promotion pays for itself in their eyes.

## 8. This Lego set hangs on the wall

![The Lego A New Hope movie poster set](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/lego.png)

For Star Wars' 50th anniversary, Lego turned the 1977 movie poster into [a $299 set built to hang on a wall](https://www.lego.com/en-us/product/a-new-hope-movie-poster-75467). [Tech Times calls it](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330588/20261112/lego-just-built-tom-jungs-1977-star-wars-poster-bricks-its-most-ambitious-anniversary-set.htm) the most ambitious anniversary set the theme has made.

**Steal this:** make a version adults can display. People who loved your product as kids will pay more to show it off as grown-ups.

## 9. Saucony bet 70% of its budget on one city

![Saucony's Rush event during London Marathon week](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/saucony.jpg)

Saucony put most of its marketing money into London races, a store and run clubs, [according to its EMEA vice president](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/targeted-turnaround-sauconys-emea-vp-on-leveraging-heritage-for-modern-growth/2026052188157), and it is bringing the plan to the US next year.

**Steal this:** win one city before you chase a country. A budget spread across everywhere is too thin to be noticed anywhere.

## 10. Fewer people drink, so this wine got smaller

![Poco Vino single-serve wine bottles](/images/blog/b2c-nov-17/poco-vino.jpg)

Only 54% of American adults drink now, so [Poco Vino sells single servings in a perfume-style bottle](https://thedieline.com/americans-are-drinking-less-so-poco-vino-bet-on-a-smaller-bottle-and-sold-2-million-right-away/), and it sold 2 million in its first year.

**Steal this:** if buyers want less, sell a smaller, nicer version.

## Your top 5 to steal for the next campaign

1. Say who your product isn't for.
2. Make your product the punchline.
3. Borrow a meme your buyers already share.
4. Sell the feature that fixes a problem your category causes.
5. Win one city before you chase a country.
